Nyne Hotels CRM database

21 tables, linked through contacts. Click any table on the map to highlight its links and jump to its columns. Row counts show what the seed file loads from the 8-hotel workbook.

How the tables connect

Each line is a foreign key, drawn from the table that holds it to the table it points at. Staff links, and the link from companies to countries, are hidden until you select one of those tables.

Select a table to see what it links to.

Automatic rules

Recording a stay

Moves the person forward: 1 stay makes them “stayed”, 2 or more “repeat guest”. A lead becomes a guest. Never moves anyone backwards, never touches agents or influencers.

Do not contact

Setting a contact to do-not-contact switches every one of their consents to opted out.

merge_contacts(keep, remove)

Moves every stay, note, date, email, phone, tag and lead to the kept record, drops a stay both records share, keeps the strictest consent so an opt-out is never lost, logs the merge, then deletes the duplicate. All or nothing.

Nights

Worked out from arrival and departure dates on each stay.

Audit columns

Every table records created, updated and deleted (when and by whom), filled in automatically from the logged-in staff member. Contact tags and the merge log only record created.

Soft delete

Deleting sets deleted_at. The row stays for history and every view hides it.

Ready-made views

contact_overview

One line per person: type, stage, primary email and phone, hotels, number of stays, last stay, email consent, tags.

marketing_email_list

People you may email right now: opted in, active, with a usable personal email. 900 people after seeding.

possible_duplicates

Pairs of contacts with the same name, email or phone, for staff to review before merging. 201 pairs after seeding.

upcoming_special_dates

Occasions in the next 60 days, with days to go, years married or age turning (when the year is known), and email consent.

feedback_overview

Every feedback response with guest, hotel and stay dates, newest first. Ratings of 1–2 are flagged for follow-up.

employee_feedback

Every guest comment about an employee, with hotel, guest, praise or criticism, and the stay rating, newest first.

employee_feedback_summary

Per employee: how many times guests praised or criticised them, overall and in the last 90 days.

property_ratings

Per hotel: number of responses, average rating overall and for the last 90 days, and how many low ratings.

property_guest_counts

Guests, stay rows and dated stays per hotel.
